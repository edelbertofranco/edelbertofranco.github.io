Thesis topics

Undergraduate (TCC and scientific initiation) and graduate. Updated 05/10/2026.

These are topics of interest with a well-defined direction, in the broad areas of Computer Networks and Cybersecurity. Other topics in these lines can be discussed. If a topic interests you, get in touch.

Cybersecurity

  • 6G: AI/ML applied to 6G security
  • 5G: 5G security with NWDAF (Network Data Analytics Function)
  • Zero Trust (NIST SP 800-207): continuous and context-aware authentication, e.g. in 5G, e-health and agentic AI systems
  • Privacy in federated learning
  • Intrusion detection systems and DPI (Deep Packet Inspection)

Computer Networks

  • Application: applications for 5G/6G
  • Link and physical: localization of people and objects via Wi-Fi (IEEE 802.11 FTM or CSI, Channel State Information)
  • 5G/6G and O-RAN: resource orchestration in the 5G Core; AI-based xApps and rApps in O-RAN
  • Other: XaaS (anything as a service)
